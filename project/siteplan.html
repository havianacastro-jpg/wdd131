Bean & Brew

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Name: Bean & Brew

Description: This name was chosen because it evokes the direct essence of specialty coffee (the "bean" and the "brew"). It represents a cozy space focused on discovering, rating, and reviewing the best local coffee shops.

Optional domain availability: bean-and-brew-guide.org

Site Purpose

The website functions as a comprehensive guide for local coffee lovers. It provides detailed reviews, quality ratings, information on coffee bean types, geographical locations, and an interactive form for visitors to submit their own recommendations and experiences.

Scenarios

Key questions that users from our target audience will look to resolve on the site:

  • Which are the best-rated coffee shops in the downtown area for working or studying?
  • How can I submit a review for a new coffee shop I just discovered?

Color Schema

The selected colors reflect the warmth, earthiness, and culture surrounding coffee:

Primary

Dark Coffee Brown (#4A3B32): Used for the main header, footer, and highlighted headings.

Secondary

Light Coffee (#D4A373): Used for borders, visual accents, and divider lines.

Accent

Soft Cream (#FAEDCD): Used for card backgrounds and highlighting content blocks.

Typography

Two fonts have been selected to maintain readability and elegance across the site:

  • Heading Font (Merriweather): Used for all primary headings (h1, h2, h3) to provide a classic and editorial feel.
  • Body Font (Roboto): Used for paragraphs, lists, and general text to ensure a clean and modern reading experience.
